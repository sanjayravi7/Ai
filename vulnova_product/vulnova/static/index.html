Method. priority_score is a provisional, rule-based sum: KEV 40 · EPSS 30 · CVSS 20 · publication_recency 10 = 100. Missing inputs score zero and are named in missing_signals; the fixed denominator is never rescaled. publication_recency is based on the CVE publication date only: it does not represent recent exploitation activity, recent KEV inclusion, or an independently verified increase in threat activity. It is not a validated predictive model and must not be read as a probability of exploitation. Absence from the KEV catalog is not evidence of no exploitation.
